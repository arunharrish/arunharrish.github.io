About
MECHANICAL ENGINEER -
FLUID AND THERMAL SYSTEMS
Modeling · Simulation · Optimization

I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate student at Georgia Tech working in computational modeling, simulation-driven design, and engineering optimization. My work focuses on CFD, thermal-fluid systems, structural analysis, and reduced-order modeling for complex engineering applications.

My recent work spans conjugate heat transfer and coolant flow analysis at LiquidPiston and wind-load prediction for tower cranes at Georgia Tech's Crane Safety Research Center, where I develop scalable CFD workflows and reduced-order models for large parametric studies.

I'm particularly interested in combining physics-based simulation with optimization and surrogate modeling to reduce computational cost while maintaining engineering accuracy. My work has involved DOE methods, Gaussian Process Regression, NSGA-II optimization, and HPC-driven CFD automation.

Before Georgia Tech, I completed my undergraduate studies at PSG College of Technology and led a 25-member Baja SAE team focused on vehicle design and integration. That experience kick-started my interest in building engineering solutions that connect analysis, performance, and real-world implementation.
